WALLET SECURITY GUIDE

Crypto Wallet Security: A Practical Guide

A safer wallet routine starts with protecting the recovery method, verifying what you install and slowing down before signing.

1. Protect your recovery phrase and private keys

A recovery phrase or private key can give control over the assets associated with a wallet. Anyone who obtains it may be able to take control. Store recovery information offline in a secure place appropriate to your needs, and never enter it into a website or share it in chat, email or support conversations.

2. Verify wallet software and browser extensions

Install wallet software only from the wallet provider’s official website or a verified app-store listing. Check the publisher, spelling and domain carefully. Search ads, unsolicited messages and look-alike domains can lead to impersonation pages.

3. Review what you sign

Before signing a transaction or message, check which app requested it, what action is being authorized and whether it matches your intent. A wallet signature is not automatically harmless because it is not a familiar token transfer.

4. Understand token approvals

Some smart-contract interactions ask a wallet to approve a token allowance or an NFT operator. Depending on the token standard and approval, a spender or operator may be able to move or manage assets within the granted permission. Read our Token Approval Guide.

5. Secure the device and accounts around the wallet

  • Use a unique password for email and exchange accounts.
  • Enable strong multi-factor authentication or passkeys where supported.
  • Keep your operating system, browser and wallet software updated.
  • Review active sessions, connected devices and account recovery settings.
  • Avoid installing remote-access software at the request of unsolicited support contacts.

6. Plan for loss or device failure

Understand how your wallet can be restored before you need to restore it. Follow official documentation and never expose your recovery phrase. Hardware wallets and separate wallets for higher-risk activity have trade-offs; assess your own needs.

This guide is educational. NexoraScope does not connect to your wallet, inspect your device, test your recovery phrase or determine whether your wallet is secure.
