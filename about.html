OUR PURPOSE

About NexoraScope

NexoraScope helps cryptocurrency users build safer digital-asset habits through clear, practical educational resources.

What we focus on

Our educational content covers account protection, wallet safety, app permissions, token approvals, phishing and crypto custody. The goal is to help readers understand common risks and know which questions to ask before taking action.

What this website currently provides

  • A short educational checkup based only on answers visitors select.
  • A practical account-security checklist that stays in the current page session.
  • Written guides explaining common wallet and account-security concepts.
  • A separate scanner that requests third-party provider indicators for public EVM addresses on supported networks.
Important limitation: The scanner does not connect to wallets, request signatures, submit transactions or revoke permissions. It displays third-party provider indicators for a public address; coverage may be incomplete, provider data may be unavailable, and results are not a security audit or certification.

How to use our information

Use these materials as a starting point, verify important details with official wallet, exchange or protocol documentation, and seek qualified advice when appropriate. Never share a private key, recovery phrase or password with anyone claiming to provide support.

Editorial approach

We aim to explain risks in plain language, distinguish general education from live product functionality, and link to primary resources where practical.
