Crypto Scam Prevention: Warning Signs to Know

Scams often create urgency, impersonate trusted services or ask you to reveal credentials or move assets. Independent verification can reduce risk.

1. Phishing websites and messages

Phishing attempts imitate wallet providers, exchanges, projects or familiar people. Check the full domain, not just the logo. Avoid signing in through links in unexpected messages; navigate using a saved bookmark or known official address.

2. Fake customer support

Scammers may reply to public posts or send direct messages pretending to be support. Be suspicious of anyone asking for a recovery phrase, private key, password, remote access or an upfront transfer to “unlock” funds. Find support through the provider’s official website or app.

3. Impersonation and giveaway scams

Fraudulent accounts may impersonate public figures, project teams or exchanges and promise guaranteed returns, giveaways or special access. Treat requests to send crypto first as a major warning sign. Verify announcements through official channels.

4. Malicious approvals and transaction prompts

A deceptive site may ask you to sign a transaction or grant a token or NFT permission broader than expected. Stop if the request is unclear. Check the application, network, recipient or spender, and action. See Understanding Token Approvals.

5. Pressure and guaranteed-profit claims

Scams often use countdowns, threats, secrecy or claims of guaranteed returns. Take time to independently verify claims rather than rushing.

If you think you interacted with a scam

  • Stop interacting with the suspicious site or account.
  • From a trusted device, review account sessions and secure affected email or exchange accounts.
  • If a wallet permission may have been granted, review it using a trusted method and understand the transaction before acting.
  • Preserve transaction hashes, URLs and messages for reporting; do not share recovery phrases.
  • Contact the relevant provider or local consumer-protection channel through independently verified details.
