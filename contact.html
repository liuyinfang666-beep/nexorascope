Before contacting a crypto service

Use the official website or app of your wallet, exchange or protocol to find its support channel. Do not trust unsolicited direct messages or search-ad results without checking the domain carefully.

Never send sensitive wallet information

NexoraScope will never need your password, private key, recovery phrase or one-time authentication code to provide educational information. Do not share these details in email, forms, social media or chat.
